About

I lead Maryland's Adversary Emulation program, which brings together two teams: MD-HACK, our bug bounty, penetration testing, and coordinated vulnerability disclosure program, and MD-ASM, our attack surface and vulnerability management program. I started MD-HACK with no team and no template and grew it into one of the premier government disclosure programs in the country. I own its scope, rules of engagement, safe harbor, and researcher relationships, and I still triage reports alongside the team.

Before Maryland I was the sole security resource for the Baltimore Ravens, and before that I spent nearly nine years at the Department of Defense, from exploitation analyst to technical director of a 200+ person joint DoD / U.S. Cyber Command organization. I think the best security programs treat researchers as partners, not adversaries.
